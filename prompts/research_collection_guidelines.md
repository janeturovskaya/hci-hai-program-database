# Research Collection Guidelines

This project is intentionally not populated with real university data yet.
Future collection prompts should prioritize:

1. Official university, program, faculty, and lab pages.
2. Explicit source URLs for every claim.
3. Direct evidence snippets that are short enough to audit.
4. Uncertainty notes when a claim is inferred, outdated, or ambiguous.
5. Manual review before processed exports are treated as canonical.

Do not add generated or guessed facts to the database.
