# Application shortlist for Evgeniia

This document turns the current Pilot 011 shortlist into a practical decision aid. It uses only repository evidence and is not a ranking. Each program below should move to the same current decision: **Prioritize for application-readiness review**.

## University of Maryland — MS Human-Computer Interaction

### Why it fits my background and goals

- Builds on existing UXR and psychology experience with a dedicated HCI master’s rather than a general design-only program.
- Adds structure in research methods, data analysis, design, UX/UI, HCI, and programming.
- Keeps both professional and research-oriented paths open through a capstone-or-thesis structure.
- Supports the goal of becoming stronger in technical, quantitative, and AI-adjacent product research.

### What it could add beyond my existing UXR and psychology experience

A stronger technical and analytical spine: programming, data analysis, HCI theory, design structure, and the option to pursue a faculty-supervised thesis instead of only applied client work.

### Best evidence of Human-AI / AI-product relevance

Pilot 011 identifies relevant elective evidence in machine learning, Generative AI in UX, social computing, data visualization, and usability testing. The Human-AI fit appears promising, but it likely depends on elective access and availability.

### Methods and technical development

The strongest evidence is the combination of HCI, UX/UI, research methods, data analysis, design, and programming in the core, with additional technical options through machine learning and data visualization.

### Applied project or research structure

UMD has one of the clearest structures in the shortlist: a two-semester client capstone or a two-semester faculty-supervised thesis.

### Main unanswered question

Are the AI/ML/Generative AI electives regularly offered and realistically accessible to HCIM students, and is direct HCIL or lab access available for master’s students?

### Current decision

**Prioritize for application-readiness review**

## How to choose among these five

- **Dedicated HCI versus information-school breadth:** UMD and Drexel are the clearest dedicated HCI/UX programs. Berkeley, UT Austin, and Michigan offer broader information-school ecosystems, which may be better for connecting UXR with AI, data, policy, product, and sociotechnical work, but may require more careful course planning.
- **Applied capstone/co-op versus thesis/research access:** Drexel appears strongest for applied professional structure through projects, capstone, portfolio, and possible co-op. UMD is the clearest for choosing between a client capstone and a faculty-supervised thesis. Berkeley has a final project, Michigan has SI 699 and internship credit, and UT Austin still needs the most evidence on applied or research structure.
- **Direct Human-AI focus versus broad AI/data electives:** Drexel and UT Austin currently have the most direct Human-AI / Human-Centered AI wording. UMD, Berkeley, and Michigan have strong AI-adjacent or AI/data options, but the practical fit may depend more on elective availability and access.
- **Technical depth versus preservation of strong qualitative/mixed-methods identity:** Berkeley and UT Austin may offer the broadest technical/data expansion. UMD and Michigan balance technical development with HCI/UX methods. Drexel may be especially useful if the priority is to keep a strong UX research and applied methods identity while adding explicit Human-AI relevance.
