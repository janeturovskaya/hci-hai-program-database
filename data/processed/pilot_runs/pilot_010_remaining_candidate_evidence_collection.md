# Pilot 010 remaining candidate evidence collection

Date accessed: 2026-06-19. This pass used official university, school, department, program, catalog, course, handbook, and career/outcomes pages only. It adds structured source rows to `data/candidates/candidate_sources.csv` and does not change canonical manual records, candidate statuses, scores, promotions, schemas, scripts, or program inventory.

## Scope and approach

The goal was broad evidence collection for the nine remaining existing candidate programs, not promotion, scoring, or ranking. Evidence summaries label direct program/pathway evidence when the source describes the candidate program, curriculum, courses, structure, capstone, or outcomes directly. They label indirect context when a source is historical, school-level, or ecosystem-level and should not be treated as a guaranteed experience for all students.

## Cornell University — MPS Information Science

- **Evidence found by category:** Existing and new official rows now document direct MPS academic planning, optional focus areas in user experience, data science, interactive technologies, and networks/crowds/markets, project-based curriculum structure, and professional relevance. Department-level rows also document HCI and Human-AI research-area context.
- **Important gaps:** Direct MPS student access to research labs, thesis mechanisms, HCI/HAI project placement, and current career outcomes by UX/AI-product job title remain incomplete. Optional focus areas should not be treated as required for every student.
- **Enough for future promotion-readiness review?** **Probably enough for a review, but not enough for promotion without gap checks.** The documented fit is strong for an information-science/UX/HAI-adjacent program, but research access and required HCI/AI coursework remain indirect or optional.

## UC Berkeley — Master of Information Management and Systems

- **Evidence found by category:** Direct MIMS rows now document a two-year full-time STEM professional master’s identity, applicant/program interest areas spanning UX, HCI, AI, NLP, data science, product management, a curriculum/final-project structure, HCI research and UX-for-AI course evidence, school-level HCI faculty/research context, and career outcomes spanning UX, product, data, ML, AI, and research roles.
- **Important gaps:** HCI/AI coursework is elective/pathway evidence rather than a required HCI sequence for all students. Master’s research-lab access remains less direct than final-project evidence.
- **Enough for future promotion-readiness review?** **Yes.** The program now has enough official evidence for a focused promotion-readiness review, with careful handling of optional/elective claims.

## UC Irvine — Master of Human-Computer Interaction and Design

- **Evidence found by category:** Direct MHCID evidence supports a lock-step cohort curriculum, learning outcomes in human behavior/cognition, user-facing software/prototyping, qualitative and quantitative user research and evaluation methods, and a final six-month capstone. Program pages also show applied industry-client/product-service work with interdisciplinary faculty.
- **Important gaps:** AI-product or Human-AI relevance was not strongly documented in the reviewed sources. The full official course catalogue should be checked to enumerate required course titles, technical depth, and any AI/ML electives.
- **Enough for future promotion-readiness review?** **Yes for dedicated HCI/design fit; not yet for AI-product fit.** MHCID has strong HCI/methods/applied-project evidence, but Human-AI evidence remains a gap.

## DePaul University — MS Human Computer Interaction

- **Evidence found by category:** Direct catalog/program evidence supports a dedicated HCI MS focused on user research, design, implementation, and evaluation for useful, easy-to-use, accessible technologies. Requirements and course descriptions document 48 degree quarter hours, in-person/online availability, user research methods, survey design/analysis, practicum, research capstone, independent study, web analytics, and UX/HCI course options.
- **Important gaps:** AI/ML or Human-AI relevance was not established from official sources reviewed in this pass. Exact required-versus-elective distribution should be handled carefully because several rows cite course options.
- **Enough for future promotion-readiness review?** **Yes for HCI/UX readiness review; uncertain for HAI-specific review.** The HCI and methods evidence is direct, but AI-product evidence is thin.

## UT Austin — MS Information Studies

- **Evidence found by category:** Direct MSIS evidence documents flexible areas of study including HCI, UX research/design, Human-Centered AI and Data Science, data engineering, information retrieval/search, and informatics. Course evidence documents Human-AI Interaction, human-centered AI design methods, psychological/societal AI impacts, UX research/design/evaluation, prompt engineering, machine learning, data wrangling, accessible UX, and search-experience coursework.
- **Important gaps:** Areas of study and courses do not prove a required pathway for every MSIS student. Applied-project, thesis, internship, or direct lab-placement evidence for MSIS students needs further official confirmation.
- **Enough for future promotion-readiness review?** **Probably yes.** The HCI/UX/HCAI/data evidence is broad and official, but review should focus on whether there is a coherent master’s pathway and documented applied/research structure.

## Arizona State University — Online MS User Experience

- **Evidence found by category:** Direct ASU Online evidence supports an online 30-credit, 10-class MS UX with 7.5-week classes. The program combines human systems engineering, graphic information technology, and technical writing/communication; covers research, design, communication, UX principles, user needs, usability testing, and evaluation; and prepares students for UX, design research, HCI, interface design, interaction design, digital visualization design, and usability testing roles.
- **Important gaps:** No strong Human-AI/AI-product or research-lab evidence was found. Catalog-level required course titles, capstone details, and technical/programming/statistics depth need follow-up.
- **Enough for future promotion-readiness review?** **Borderline.** Enough exists for an online UX candidate review, but not enough for a Human-AI or research-intensive promotion case.

## Rochester Institute of Technology — Human-Computer Interaction MS

- **Evidence found by category:** Direct program evidence supports a dedicated HCI MS covering design methods, evaluation, and implementation of interactive computing systems, grounded in psychology and human behavior, and aimed at UX researchers and computing professionals. Official handbook/historical pages add capstone sequencing and lab-based capstone context.
- **Important gaps:** Current catalog-level capstone/thesis options, exact required course sequence, AI/HAI relevance, career outcomes, and current lab access should be verified. Historical lab/capstone evidence should be treated as context until current requirements confirm it.
- **Enough for future promotion-readiness review?** **Probably yes for dedicated HCI fit after current-catalog checks.** Strong HCI identity exists; HAI and current applied/research details need more support.

## Northwestern University — MS Artificial Intelligence

- **Evidence found by category:** Direct MSAI evidence strongly supports AI-system/product relevance, including AI systems that partner with human users and integrate into workflows, business, and human interactions. Curriculum evidence documents required AI, machine learning, interactive AI systems, and human cognition; course evidence documents HCI; overview evidence supports product/team leadership outcomes.
- **Important gaps:** UX research methods, human-subjects research training, capstone/client structure, and HCI coursework requirement status need confirmation. This is an AI program with HCI/Human-AI elements, not a dedicated HCI/UX degree.
- **Enough for future promotion-readiness review?** **Yes for Human-AI/AI-product adjacency; uncertain for HCI/UXR fit.** Review should test whether the program meets the database’s HCI/HAI target rather than general AI excellence.

## University of Chicago — MA Computational Social Science

- **Evidence found by category:** Direct MACSS evidence documents foundations in data science and programming, social-science research, AI/ML relevance in computational social science, 18 graduate seminars, an MA thesis, boot camp and placement exam components, internship partnerships, large-data analysis, modeling, programming, qualitative and quantitative research skills, and strong data/research career outcomes.
- **Important gaps:** HCI, UX research, Human-AI interaction, product design, and applied technology evaluation are indirect at best in the reviewed sources. The program’s fit depends on computational social/behavioral research relevance rather than direct HCI/HAI training.
- **Enough for future promotion-readiness review?** **Only for an indirect-fit review.** It has strong computational/social research evidence but weak documented HCI/HAI/AI-product alignment.
