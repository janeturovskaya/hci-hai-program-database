# Pilot 004: dimension-specific scoring evidence

Date: 2026-06-17

## Claim category support added

This pilot added optional `claim_category` support to canonical evidence rows and candidate source rows. The field identifies the kind of claim supported by a source, including scoring dimensions such as `hci_strength`, `hai_strength`, `faculty_lab_fit`, `research_access_for_masters`, `quant_data_ai_curriculum`, `methods_fit`, `career_relevance`, and `practical_fit`, plus practical-detail categories such as `cost`, `deadline`, `prerequisites`, `online_available`, `duration`, and `capstone`.

Blank categories remain valid for legacy rows. New or touched evidence from this pilot was categorized when the snippet supported a specific claim.

## Evidence collected

Evidence collection was limited to the three promoted canonical pilot programs:

* Carnegie Mellon University — Master of Human-Computer Interaction
* University of Washington — Master of Science in Human Centered Design & Engineering
* Georgia Institute of Technology — MS Program in Human-Computer Interaction

Added or categorized official evidence for HCI strength, HAI strength, faculty/lab fit, research access where available, AI/data/quant fit where available, methods fit where available, career relevance, and practical fit. Several rows rely on official department, faculty, research, or program pages and include uncertainty notes when the evidence is ecosystem-level rather than direct program access evidence.

## Weak dimensions remaining

* CMU MHCI still lacks direct promoted evidence for master's research access and specific quant/data/AI curriculum.
* UW HCDE MS still lacks methods-specific evidence strong enough for a numeric methods score in this pilot; AI/data evidence is department-level and should be tied to specific courses before broader scoring.
* Georgia Tech MS-HCI still lacks methods-specific evidence strong enough for a numeric methods score; faculty/lab and AI evidence should be connected more directly to master's student pathways.

## More confidently scored dimensions

* HCI strength is now well supported for all three promoted programs.
* UW and Georgia Tech have stronger evidence for research ecosystem and access than before, though some evidence remains indirect.
* Career relevance and practical fit are stronger where official program pages describe outcomes, capstones, credits, internships, or delivery details.

## Evidence still missing before broader scoring

Before expanding scoring beyond this pilot, collect official course-level evidence for methods, AI/data/quant curriculum, and master's research or lab access. Cost and deadline evidence should also be refreshed from current official admissions and tuition pages.

## Rubric usability

The rubric was usable for conservative exploratory scoring. `IE` remains important: category support makes evidence easier to audit, but it does not eliminate the need to leave dimensions insufficiently evidenced when official sources are broad, indirect, or missing.
