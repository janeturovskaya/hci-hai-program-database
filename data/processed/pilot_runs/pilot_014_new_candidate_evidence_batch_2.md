# Pilot 014 — New Candidate Evidence Batch 2

Date accessed: 2026-06-19  
Scope: official-source evidence for the six remaining candidate programs added in PR #28. This report summarizes only candidate evidence added to `data/candidates/candidate_sources.csv`; it does not change candidate rows, statuses, scores, schemas, scripts, tests, canonical records, or manual records.

## Bentley University — MS Human Factors in Information Design

- **Methods / UX research development:** Strong direct program and catalog evidence. Bentley frames HFID around UX, behavioral science, human behavior, human factors, information products, user-interface design, and measurable experience design.
- **AI, HAI, or technology-product relevance:** Moderate-to-strong contextual evidence. The program page highlights AI, ethics, emerging technology, and faculty work at the intersection of AI and HCI, including AI-driven interfaces and extended reality.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Some technical evidence from the program framing and alumni testimonial references to technical courses such as Python and databases, but the current source rows emphasize AI/HCI context and human-factors methods rather than a verified required technical sequence.
- **Applied project / studio / research access:** Strong applied evidence. Bentley describes capstone collaborations, experience design research, design challenges, and class projects with organizations.
- **Career relevance:** Strong direct catalog evidence for UX research and design, customer experience, and product management careers.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Confirm which technical, analytics, AI, or programming courses are required versus elective, and whether all students complete the same capstone or client-project structure.

## Pace University — MS Human Centered Design

- **Methods / UX research development:** Strong direct catalog evidence. Required coursework includes UX design, HCI, research methods for UX, human factors and usability metrics, and prototyping.
- **AI, HAI, or technology-product relevance:** Limited current AI-specific evidence. The program is clearly HCI/technology-product aligned through computer science, information systems, cognitive science, and interaction design, but official AI-product or Human-AI coursework was not identified in the added rows.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Stronger than many design-school options. Pace lists development concentration electives in mobile application development, mobile web content/development, coding, and information systems design/development.
- **Applied project / studio / research access:** Strong direct evidence. The 30-credit program requires either a UX capstone project or a product development project, and students may take both with one counted as an elective.
- **Career relevance:** Good direct evidence. Pace says graduates gain skills to become HCI practitioners, researchers, and leaders.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Determine how often students take the development concentration and whether AI, data, or advanced research opportunities are available within the HCD plan.

## University of Colorado Boulder — MS Creative Technology & Design

- **Methods / UX research development:** Moderate evidence. The CTD program is not a conventional HCI/UX research degree, but ATLS Design Methods covers observation, cultural probes, ethnography, problem finding, design thinking, wicked problems, iteration, alternative generation, and assessment.
- **AI, HAI, or technology-product relevance:** Moderate contextual evidence. ATLAS has official generative-AI/AR faculty and alumni context, including an automated AR system with dynamic visual guidance, but this is not shown as a required MS pathway.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Strong emerging-technology and build orientation. CU Boulder describes CTD as blending engineering, computer science, creative problem solving, and design through project-based curricula that build physical and digital solutions.
- **Applied project / studio / research access:** Strong opportunity evidence. ATLAS says MS students can take classes with faculty, work on independent projects, and join research labs or creative spaces, though placement is not guaranteed.
- **Career relevance:** Strong for design-technology and creative-technology roles. The Creative Industries track is a 33-credit professional MS preparing students for technology and design roles, with flexible scheduling and many students working part-time.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Clarify which track-specific courses are required for all CTD MS students, and how much UX research or HAI work is accessible to master’s students versus being broader ATLAS ecosystem context.

## Brandeis University — MS User-Centered Design

- **Methods / UX research development:** Strong direct evidence. Brandeis says the online MS explores how people think, behave, and interact with technology, with required courses in UX design, human factors psychology and ergonomics, information architecture, research methods, design ideation, prototyping, and capstone.
- **AI, HAI, or technology-product relevance:** Strong elective evidence. Brandeis lists AI for Human-Centered Design and Technology Futures, plus related analytics and development electives.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Moderate-to-strong elective evidence. Electives include information visualization, survey methods, web development technologies, foundations of data science and analytics, statistics and data analysis, and business intelligence.
- **Applied project / studio / research access:** Strong direct evidence for a required capstone producing a product or system prototype, plus an optional internship.
- **Career relevance:** Strong direct evidence. Brandeis lists UX designer, UX researcher, information architect, UI designer, web developer, and related roles; the program is 100% online, 30 credits, STEM-designated, and part- or full-time.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** AI, analytics, web development, and internship evidence is elective; verify frequency and accessibility before comparing technical depth against more computing-centered programs.

## California College of the Arts — MDes Interaction Design

- **Methods / UX research development:** Moderate-to-strong design-practice evidence. CCA emphasizes systems thinking, design leadership, iteration, collaboration, prototyping, building, measurable social impact, and relationships among people, products, and environments.
- **AI, HAI, or technology-product relevance:** Limited AI-specific evidence. Technology-product relevance is explicit through human-centered technology and the San Francisco/Silicon Valley context, but no direct AI or Human-AI coursework was found in the added official evidence.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Moderate emerging-product evidence. The program is STEM-designated, one year, and centered on human-centered technology, prototyping, and building, but current evidence does not establish programming, analytics, or quantitative requirements.
- **Applied project / studio / research access:** Strong studio/design evidence from the one-year intensive structure, concept-to-final-product language, prototyping, and Bay Area research-driven design practice context.
- **Career relevance:** Strong design-career evidence. CCA says the program prepares students for interaction design across industries and notes graduate success in research-driven design practices.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Need official curriculum/course-level evidence to distinguish required research methods, technical build requirements, and any thesis/project requirement from general program positioning.

## Savannah College of Art and Design — MFA UX Design

- **Methods / UX research development:** Strong direct curriculum evidence. Required or listed MFA courses include Theory of UX Design, Methods of Contextual Research, Cognitive Human Factors for Designers, Usability Testing and Evaluation, and Information Architecture for Designers.
- **AI, HAI, or technology-product relevance:** Limited direct Human-AI evidence. SCAD has strong UX, service design, front-end, prototyping, coding, and electronics evidence, but no AI-specific MFA requirement was identified in the added official sources.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Strong for design-technology production. The curriculum includes Front-end Design for User Experience, Prototyping Coding: Proof of Concept, Modeling Electronics for Designers, service visualization, and electives.
- **Applied project / studio / research access:** Strong direct evidence. SCAD requires two MFA thesis courses: research/synthesis/insight and design/validation/execution, and offers options such as user-centered research for business, SCADpro collaboration, or graduate internship.
- **Career relevance:** Good implicit career relevance through the named UX MFA curriculum and applied thesis/internship/SCADpro options; additional official career-outcomes evidence would strengthen this category.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Verify whether AI, analytics, or advanced research-lab opportunities exist for UX MFA students, and gather official careers/outcomes pages if available.

## Compact comparison

- **Programs that appear strongest for advanced UX research and design practice:** Bentley, Brandeis, Pace, and SCAD currently have the clearest direct methods evidence. Bentley is especially strong on human factors/UX research and client-facing experience design; Brandeis and Pace have explicit required research/usability/prototyping curricula; SCAD has contextual research, usability evaluation, cognitive human factors, and thesis validation.
- **Programs strongest for technical or emerging-technology development:** CU Boulder and SCAD appear strongest for build-oriented and emerging-technology development. CU Boulder emphasizes engineering, computer science, physical/digital solutions, labs, and creative technology. SCAD has front-end UX, prototyping/coding, electronics, service visualization, and thesis execution. Brandeis and Pace have meaningful elective technical depth, but current evidence marks much of it as elective.
- **Programs primarily design/studio-oriented rather than research-oriented:** CCA and CU Boulder appear most studio/creative-technology oriented. SCAD is also design-school oriented, but its official MFA curriculum includes more explicit contextual research, usability evaluation, and thesis research/validation than CCA evidence currently shows.
- **Online or flexible options worth comparing:** Brandeis is the clearest online/flexible option: 100% online, 30 credits, part- or full-time, finishable in 1–2 years. Bentley remains worth comparing because the candidate row indicates in-person or online and the program has strong HFID evidence, but modality should be reconfirmed in official source rows if promotion is considered. CU Boulder notes schedule flexibility and many students working part-time, but it is not an online program in current evidence.
- **Programs least aligned with Human-AI / AI-product research based on current evidence:** CCA, Pace, and SCAD currently have the weakest direct AI/HAI evidence. They may be technology-product or UX-design relevant, but the added official sources do not establish AI-product research depth. CU Boulder and Bentley have stronger AI/emerging-technology context, while Brandeis has the clearest named AI-for-HCD elective evidence.
