# Pilot 016: Targeted HAI/HCAI landscape gap fill

Date: 2026-06-19.

This discovery round adds exactly 10 lightweight U.S. candidate programs intended to fill Human-AI, Human-Centered AI, human factors, HCI, and research-access gaps left after the expanded candidate comparison. It uses only official university, school, department, catalog, program, course, or official-news pages. All added records remain `needs_review`; this round does not rank programs or add admissions, deadlines, tuition, funding, or detailed course-plan research.

## Added programs

### Syracuse University — MS Applied Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence

- **Why it was added:** The program is explicitly branded around applied human-centered AI and ethical AI use.
- **What it contributes beyond the existing candidate landscape:** It adds a lightweight, direct HCAI-labeled iSchool option rather than another generic HCI or UX program.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** The official iSchool page describes the Applied Human Centered AI master's as training students in AI technologies while staying grounded in ethical and human-centered principles.
- **Main uncertainty for later review:** Whether the program includes direct human-subject evaluation, mixed-methods product research, or master’s research/lab access rather than primarily applied AI portfolio work.

### Stevens Institute of Technology — MS Human-Centered AI

- **Why it was added:** Stevens has a new MS specifically named Human-Centered AI.
- **What it contributes beyond the existing candidate landscape:** It adds a direct HCAI program with explicit design, psychology, ethics, AI/ML, and people-centered evaluation language.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** Official Stevens news describes a Fall 2026 human-centered AI master's that blends AI/ML technical foundations with interdisciplinary expertise for useful, accessible, and responsible AI systems.
- **Main uncertainty for later review:** Because the program is new, catalog-level requirements, project structure, and actual master’s access to HAI research need confirmation.

### George Mason University — MS Applied Information Technology with Human-AI Interaction pathway

- **Why it was added:** The MS AIT catalog includes Human-AI Interaction, Interactive Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, and Interaction Design and Accessibility in the applied AI/IT course ecosystem.
- **What it contributes beyond the existing candidate landscape:** It adds a technical applied-IT pathway where HAI appears directly at course level rather than only as contextual faculty research.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** The official catalog lists AIT 618 Human-AI Interaction and related applied AI, interactive ML/AI, and interaction-design/accessibility coursework.
- **Main uncertainty for later review:** Whether these courses form a coherent, advisor-supported master’s pathway and whether HAI evaluation methods are substantial enough for the target profile.

### Penn State University — MS Informatics

- **Why it was added:** The MS Informatics program frames information and technology as interacting with people, organizations, and the world community.
- **What it contributes beyond the existing candidate landscape:** It adds another iSchool/informatics route for socio-technical and research-oriented AI/data exploration without claiming a dedicated HAI degree.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** Official program and bulletin pages describe an interdisciplinary MS focused on how information and technology affect, and are affected by, people and organizations.
- **Main uncertainty for later review:** Direct Human-AI evaluation methods, master’s lab access, and a concrete HCI/AI course pathway remain unknown.

### University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign — MS Information Management

- **Why it was added:** UIUC’s MSIM combines technical information/data skills with organizational and social challenges.
- **What it contributes beyond the existing candidate landscape:** It adds a high-capacity information-management route with human-centered design, data analytics, and research-method outcomes.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** The official catalog includes outcomes for human-centered design and systems, data analytics, and addressing research questions or real-world problems with appropriate methods.
- **Main uncertainty for later review:** HAI-specific coursework, UX research depth, and practical master’s access to labs or applied HAI projects are not yet established.

### Emory University — MS Computer Science

- **Why it was added:** Emory’s CS MS explicitly allows focus in AI, NLP/intelligent systems, visualization, and HCI.
- **What it contributes beyond the existing candidate landscape:** It adds a computer-science option where official research-area evidence directly includes Human AI Interaction, recommender/dialog systems, visualization, bias, AI ethics, and future of work.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** Emory CS official pages identify HCI strengths including Human AI Interaction and AI/computing ethics alongside MS focus areas in machine learning, NLP/intelligent systems, visualization, and HCI.
- **Main uncertainty for later review:** Whether MS students can realistically join HAI/HCI research and whether the curriculum supports mixed-methods human evaluation rather than primarily technical CS study.

### UNC Charlotte — MS Artificial Intelligence

- **Why it was added:** UNC Charlotte is launching a dedicated MS in Artificial Intelligence with ethical and teamwork/communication signals.
- **What it contributes beyond the existing candidate landscape:** It adds a dedicated AI master’s connected to institutional AI research and a university AI institute, useful as a technical/data gap-fill candidate.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** The official program page describes training in planning, design, implementation, testing, and management of AI systems with ethical considerations and communication/teamwork abilities.
- **Main uncertainty for later review:** Direct HAI/HCI methods, user-centered evaluation, and master’s lab access are still unknown.

### Clark University — MS Computer Science with HCI / Intelligent Systems and AI concentrations

- **Why it was added:** Clark’s MSCS offers both HCI and Intelligent Systems and AI Solutions Design concentrations.
- **What it contributes beyond the existing candidate landscape:** It adds a smaller CS program where a student might combine HCI and AI concentration evidence in one degree search.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** Official program and catalog pages list Human-Computer Interaction and Intelligent Systems and AI Solutions Design concentrations.
- **Main uncertainty for later review:** The depth of mixed-methods UX research, HAI evaluation, and graduate research access remains unclear.

### Michigan Technological University — MS Applied Cognitive Science and Human Factors

- **Why it was added:** The program is a research-intensive human factors route focused on the interface of humans and technology.
- **What it contributes beyond the existing candidate landscape:** It adds a cognitive-science/human-factors option with strong human-machine systems and research-methods orientation rather than another iSchool or design program.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** Official Michigan Tech pages describe a research-intensive program optimizing performance, health, and safety at the interface of humans and technology and improving human-machine systems.
- **Main uncertainty for later review:** AI-specific Human-AI work, software-product UX relevance, and master’s-level lab fit need confirmation.

### Grand Valley State University — MS Human-Centered Computing

- **Why it was added:** GVSU’s MS HCC directly combines UX, design thinking, software development, usability evaluation, prototyping, and exploration of AI/AR/VR.
- **What it contributes beyond the existing candidate landscape:** It adds a newer, lightweight HCC option with explicit mixed-methods user research and emerging-technology language.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** Official program and catalog pages describe design thinking, UX, software development, usability evaluation, prototyping, AI/AR/VR, user needs, interfaces, and mixed-methods user research.
- **Main uncertainty for later review:** Program maturity, direct HAI evaluation depth, and master’s research or lab access remain unknown.

## What this discovery round did not add

This round did not add a numeric ranking or promote any candidate. It also did not establish admissions fit, application readiness, costs, deadlines, funding, course plans, or career outcomes.

Remaining gaps are still significant:

- Direct Human-AI evaluation methods are unknown or only lightly evidenced for many additions, especially Penn State, UIUC, UNC Charlotte, Clark, Michigan Tech, and GVSU.
- Master’s lab access remains uncertain across the round. Official pages often show research context, but they rarely confirm whether MS students can join labs, complete assistantships, or anchor a thesis/capstone in HAI work.
- Strong mixed-methods UX research evidence is clearest for GVSU and Michigan Tech, somewhat present at UIUC and Clark, and still uncertain for the AI-first programs at Syracuse, Stevens, GMU, Emory, and UNC Charlotte.
- Several additions are new or broad programs. Stevens and UNC Charlotte especially need later catalog and implementation checks because their AI programs are launch-stage or very recent.
- The round intentionally did not broaden into non-U.S. programs, doctoral programs, certificates, bootcamps, or generic data-science/AI programs without a human-centered, HAI, HCI, human-factors, or research-access signal.
