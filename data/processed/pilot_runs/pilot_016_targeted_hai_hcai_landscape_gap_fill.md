# Pilot 016: Targeted HAI/HCAI landscape gap fill

Date: 2026-06-19.

This pilot is a final targeted candidate-discovery round, not a broad UX-program search. It adds lightweight intake evidence for U.S.-based graduate programs that fill gaps in Human-AI Interaction, Human-Centered AI, responsible AI with user research/design/evaluation relevance, AI-product evaluation, sociotechnical AI, direct research/project access, or rigorous CS/data/engineering-adjacent technical depth. It uses official university, school, department, program, catalog, news, or research pages only.

The round also checked Pilot 015 for `cand_prog_umbc_hcc_ms`. UMBC was present only as a brief named mention in the new-program impact section, not as its own contextual-option entry. Pilot 015 was therefore corrected by adding UMBC as a contextual option, using existing repository evidence only and adding no new UMBC sources.

1. Syracuse University — MS in Applied Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence.
2. Stevens Institute of Technology — Human-Centered AI, M.S.
3. George Mason University — Applied Information Technology, MS, with HCI / Human-AI Interaction pathway evidence.
4. Penn State University — M.S. in Informatics.
5. University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign — MS in Information Management.
6. Emory University — MS in Computer Science.
7. University of North Carolina at Charlotte — Artificial Intelligence, M.S.
8. Clark University — Computer Science, M.S., with HCI / Intelligent Systems and AI Solutions Design concentrations.
9. Michigan Technological University — Applied Cognitive Science and Human Factors MS.
10. Grand Valley State University — Human-Centered Computing M.S.

## New candidate intake notes

### Syracuse University — MS in Applied Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence

- **Why added:** This is a named applied Human-Centered AI master’s housed in an iSchool, directly addressing the landscape gap between generic AI programs and UX/HCI programs with only elective AI evidence.
- **Distinctive value versus existing candidates:** Compared with existing HCI and UX programs, Syracuse adds a program identity centered on applied HCAI, ethics, generative AI, hands-on projects, and portfolio work rather than treating AI as an elective add-on.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** The official program page says the master’s helps students master cutting-edge AI technologies while staying true to ethical and human-centered principles, and that students conclude with hands-on projects and an application portfolio.
- **Most important question for later review:** How much user research, human-subjects evaluation, HCI theory, and AI-product evaluation are required rather than implied by human-centered framing?

### Stevens Institute of Technology — Human-Centered AI, M.S.

- **Why added:** Stevens directly lists a Human-Centered AI, M.S., and official university news frames the program around responsible, accessible, useful AI systems.
- **Distinctive value versus existing candidates:** It adds a specialized HCAI degree from a technical institute, potentially stronger for AI/ML foundations than most UX-oriented programs while retaining product ethics and UX-research relevance.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** Stevens lists Human-Centered AI, M.S. among Computer Science graduate programs; official news says the new master’s blends AI/ML technical foundations with interdisciplinary expertise for useful, accessible, responsible systems.
- **Most important question for later review:** Once the Fall 2026 program is fully documented, what are the required courses, project structures, human-centered evaluation requirements, and research/lab access mechanisms?

### George Mason University — Applied Information Technology, MS, with HCI / Human-AI Interaction pathway evidence

- **Why added:** GMU fills a gap for a technical applied-IT master’s with explicit Human-AI Interaction, HCI, interactive machine learning, ethics, visualization, capstone, and research-project options.
- **Distinctive value versus existing candidates:** Unlike generic HCI programs with limited AI proof, the catalog explicitly places Human-AI Interaction and Interactive Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence within the MS option space.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** The official catalog lists HCI concentration courses and electives including Human-AI Interaction, Interactive Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, Information Technology, Ethics, and Society, Interactive Visualization and Data Analytics, Capstone Seminar, and Research Project. A catalog course description for Human-AI Interaction says it covers designing, building, and evaluating AI-powered tools and includes HAI principles plus front-end/back-end technical skills.
- **Most important question for later review:** Can a student with strong UXR/psychology background but limited IT coursework realistically complete this pathway and use the capstone or research project for AI-product evaluation?

### Penn State University — M.S. in Informatics

- **Why added:** Penn State adds an information-science pathway with HCI, CSCW, data mining, and possible thesis evidence, filling a research-access and sociotechnical-computing gap.
- **Distinctive value versus existing candidates:** Compared with professional UX programs, it looks more like a research-capable informatics route for HCI/social computing/data depth.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** The official College of IST page describes the M.S. in Informatics as a 30-credit degree and lists Human-Computer Interaction: The User and Technology. The graduate bulletin confirms M.S. Informatics at University Park and shows HCI and data-mining coursework in related graduate planning context.
- **Most important question for later review:** What is the current thesis/non-thesis structure for standalone M.S. students, and which HCI, CSCW, AI/data, and research-methods courses are realistically available?

### University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign — MS in Information Management

- **Why added:** Illinois adds an iSchool MS with Human-Centered Systems Design & Development, Data Science and Analytics, and human-centered AI/data research context.
- **Distinctive value versus existing candidates:** It strengthens the information-school pathway set with a STEM information-management degree that can potentially combine HCI, data science, human-centered systems, and responsible/human-centered AI research.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** The official MSIM page lists pathways in Data Science and Analytics and Human-Centered Systems Design & Development. The iSchool HCI research area highlights human-centered AI design and human-AI collective intelligence context.
- **Most important question for later review:** Can MSIM students access HCI/HCAI courses, labs, assistantships, or applied projects, or is the human-centered AI evidence primarily faculty/research-area context?

### Emory University — MS in Computer Science

- **Why added:** Emory adds a rigorous CS-adjacent route with AI, NLP, data science, visualization, HCI, and Human-AI Interaction research evidence.
- **Distinctive value versus existing candidates:** It is not a UX program; its value is technical AI/data depth with a credible HCI/Human-AI research ecosystem for an experienced UX researcher who wants stronger computational literacy.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** Emory CS says MS courses are offered in data, machine learning, AI, NLP, and HCI. Its HCI research-area page names Human AI Interaction, AI/computing ethics, recommender systems, information visualization and bias, future of work, and related strengths.
- **Most important question for later review:** Does the MS provide thesis/project access or lab participation suitable for applied AI/UX research, and how much HCI/user-study training can a master’s student take?

### University of North Carolina at Charlotte — Artificial Intelligence, M.S.

- **Why added:** UNC Charlotte adds a new dedicated AI master’s with direct Human-Centered AI, social technology design, visualization, ethics, and AI lifecycle evidence.
- **Distinctive value versus existing candidates:** It is a technical AI degree but has a clearer human-centered/evaluation-adjacent course signal than many generic AI programs.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** Official program materials say the MS covers planning, design, implementation, testing, and management of AI systems, core mathematics and AI algorithms, the AI software lifecycle, ethics, communication, and teamwork. Additional official materials list Human-Centered AI, Social Technology Design, and visualization courses.
- **Most important question for later review:** As a launch-year program, which courses will be available immediately, and can students build a project around human-centered AI or AI-product evaluation?

### Clark University — Computer Science, M.S., with HCI / Intelligent Systems and AI Solutions Design concentrations

- **Why added:** Clark adds a technical CS pathway explicitly framed around intelligent automation, data-driven decision-making, human-AI interaction, human-centered design, and HCI concentration options.
- **Distinctive value versus existing candidates:** It is a compact CS/data/AI route with direct human-AI language, potentially useful for a UXR professional seeking technical depth without choosing a pure design-school program.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** The official program page says the MS prepares students at the intersection of intelligent automation, data-driven decision-making, and human-AI interaction. The catalog lists concentrations in Intelligent Systems and AI Solutions Design, Data Intelligence, Human-Computer Interaction, and Software Engineering.
- **Most important question for later review:** What project, thesis, practicum, or lab structure exists, and how substantial is the HCI/evaluation-methods training?

### Michigan Technological University — Applied Cognitive Science and Human Factors MS

- **Why added:** Michigan Tech adds a research-intensive human-factors pathway for human-machine systems, automation, robotics, and technology interfaces.
- **Distinctive value versus existing candidates:** It strengthens the engineering/human-factors side of the landscape, especially for human-machine/automation evaluation, without being a generic UX program.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** Michigan Tech describes the MS/PhD in Applied Cognitive Science and Human Factors as research-intensive and focused on optimizing performance, health, and safety at the interface of humans and technology. Its graduate-school page says the curriculum develops scholars and practitioners who improve human-machine systems through theoretically grounded instruction and technological design, including automation and robotics contexts.
- **Most important question for later review:** How much of the MS can be oriented toward AI-product, automation, robotics, or digital UX evaluation, and what thesis/lab access is typical for master’s students?

### Grand Valley State University — Human-Centered Computing M.S.

- **Why added:** GVSU adds a human-centered computing program that combines UX, software development, AI/AR/VR exposure, usability evaluation, prototyping, mixed-methods user research, and final-project evidence.
- **Distinctive value versus existing candidates:** It fills a regional HCC/software-development gap between UX-only online programs and highly selective flagship HCI programs.
- **Strongest direct evidence:** GVSU says the MS combines design thinking, UX, and software development and explores technologies like AI and AR/VR through hands-on projects. The catalog says students develop expertise in user needs, UX/UI design, mixed-methods user research, and innovative technologies.
- **Most important question for later review:** How technically deep is the AI/software curriculum, and what is the required final project’s scope for AI-product evaluation or applied HCI research?

## What this discovery round did not add

- It did not add generic UX, interaction-design, certificate, boot-camp, undergraduate, or non-U.S. programs.
- It did not add broad AI master’s programs that lacked credible human-centered, interaction, evaluation, responsible-AI, or sociotechnical evidence.
- It did not deeply research admissions requirements, tuition, funding, deadlines, full course plans, faculty lists, career outcomes, or numeric scores.
- It did not resolve which newly added programs should enter the application-readiness shortlist; all new rows are deliberately marked `needs_review`.
- It did not close the remaining evidence gap around master’s-level lab access. Several programs have strong research-area evidence, but later review must verify whether master’s students can actually join labs, complete theses, or anchor capstone/practicum projects in Human-AI or AI-product evaluation.
