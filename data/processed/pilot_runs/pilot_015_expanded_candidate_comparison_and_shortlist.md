# Pilot 015: Expanded candidate comparison and shortlist

Date: 2026-06-19.

This is a decision-oriented synthesis of the current candidate landscape after the additions reviewed in pilots 013 and 014. It uses only evidence already in the repository, especially prior candidate and pilot reports. It does not add sources, change candidate records, alter scores, promote programs, modify generated reports, or create a numeric ranking.

The comparison is framed around Evgeniia's actual goal: build on substantial UX research and psychology experience rather than repeat entry-level UX training; develop Human-AI, Human-Centered AI, or AI-product evaluation capability; strengthen technical, quantitative, data, and AI literacy; retain meaningful qualitative and mixed-methods research strength; and preserve realistic paths into applied AI/UX research, product research, or research-adjacent roles.

## 1. Updated shortlist

### Application-readiness shortlist

These programs should receive the next application-readiness pass. The group is not ranked; each enters because repository evidence currently supports a plausible advanced path across methods, technical/AI growth, applied work or research access, and career relevance.

#### University of Maryland — MS Human-Computer Interaction

- **What it adds beyond Evgeniia's background:** UMD adds a dedicated HCI structure with programming, data analysis, HCI, UX/UI, design, and research methods rather than only more UX practice.
- **Best evidence for methods + technical/AI development:** Pilot 011 identifies strong direct evidence for HCI, UX, UI, research methods, data analysis, design, programming, and electives such as machine learning, data visualization, social computing, usability testing, and Generative AI in UX.
- **Project, thesis, capstone, practicum, or research access:** UMD has unusually clear structural evidence for either a two-semester client capstone or a two-semester faculty-supervised thesis.
- **Biggest remaining uncertainty:** Whether AI/ML/Generative AI electives are regularly offered and accessible to HCIM students, and whether master’s students can access HCIL or other labs in practice.

#### Drexel University — MS Human-Computer Interaction and User Experience

- **What it adds beyond Evgeniia's background:** Drexel adds explicit Human-AI and AI-age UX framing on top of qualitative and quantitative UX research, making it less likely to duplicate only entry-level UX training.
- **Best evidence for methods + technical/AI development:** Pilot 011 reports direct evidence for qualitative and quantitative research methods, empirical methods, prototyping, evaluation, accessibility, human-AI interaction coursework, UX for the AI age, and a human-centered AI elective pathway.
- **Project, thesis, capstone, practicum, or research access:** Evidence supports hands-on projects, teamwork, collaborative capstone, portfolio development, and graduate co-op for eligible on-campus full-time students.
- **Biggest remaining uncertainty:** The exact current required course distribution, how reliably the human-centered AI pathway can be completed, and whether master’s students have meaningful lab access beyond applied coursework.

#### UC Berkeley — MIMS

- **What it adds beyond Evgeniia's background:** Berkeley adds information-school breadth across AI, data, NLP, product, HCI, and research-oriented roles, potentially strengthening the bridge from UXR to AI-product evaluation and sociotechnical research.
- **Best evidence for methods + technical/AI development:** Pilot 011 identifies strong evidence across UX/HCI interests, AI, NLP, data science, ML/AI outcomes, product management, UX-for-AI coursework, and HCI research context.
- **Project, thesis, capstone, practicum, or research access:** The repository supports a final-project structure; direct master’s lab or research assistantship access remains less clear.
- **Biggest remaining uncertainty:** Which HCI, AI, and UX-for-AI courses are consistently available to MIMS students, and whether master’s research access is realistic beyond the final project.

#### UT Austin — MS Information Studies

- **What it adds beyond Evgeniia's background:** UT Austin adds one of the clearest Human-Centered AI and data-science profiles in the candidate pool while retaining HCI, UX research/design/evaluation, accessible UX, and search experience evidence.
- **Best evidence for methods + technical/AI development:** Pilot 011 reports evidence for Human-Centered AI and Data Science, Human-AI Interaction, psychological and societal AI impacts, prompt engineering, machine learning, data wrangling, data engineering, information retrieval/search, UX research/design/evaluation, and human-centered design methods.
- **Project, thesis, capstone, practicum, or research access:** This is the weak point: pilot 011 found insufficient evidence for an MSIS-specific capstone, thesis, practicum, internship, or direct lab-placement structure.
- **Biggest remaining uncertainty:** Whether an MSIS student can assemble a coherent HCI/UX/HCAI course plan with a concrete project, thesis, practicum, internship, or RA structure.

#### University of Michigan — MSI User Experience Research and Design pathway

- **What it adds beyond Evgeniia's background:** Michigan adds information-school technical breadth and applied project work while preserving a named UX research and design pathway.
- **Best evidence for methods + technical/AI development:** Pilot 011 identifies user needs, problem definition, research documentation, prototyping, information architecture, usability evaluation, programming core, and optional statistics, databases, interactive applications, AR/VR, IoT, Gen AI and UX, and Vibe Coding.
- **Project, thesis, capstone, practicum, or research access:** Evidence supports SI 699 as a semester-long UXRD project and an internship-credit structure; thesis and direct lab access need more confirmation.
- **Biggest remaining uncertainty:** UXRD-specific outcomes, actual availability of Gen AI or related technical courses, and whether research/lab access is realistic for master’s students.

#### Iowa State University — MS Human Computer Interaction

- **What it adds beyond Evgeniia's background:** Iowa State adds an interdepartmental HCI structure with stronger technical and evaluation evidence than many UX-only options, while keeping HCI career and doctoral-study paths visible.
- **Best evidence for methods + technical/AI development:** Pilot 013 reports usability evaluation, evaluation instruments, usability sessions, data analysis, reporting, HCI research or industry-practice topics, computational-device design and use, programming-related topics, computational perception, language parsing, automated systems, and emerging HCI research.
- **Project, thesis, capstone, practicum, or research access:** The MS offers thesis and creative-component options, including a capstone route with coursework plus a capstone project.

#### Brandeis University — MS User-Centered Design

- **What it adds beyond Evgeniia's background:** Brandeis adds a flexible online route that still has explicit research methods, human factors psychology, capstone, and named AI-for-HCD elective evidence, making it more advanced than a generic online UX credential.
- **Best evidence for methods + technical/AI development:** Pilot 014 reports required UX design, human factors psychology and ergonomics, information architecture, research methods, design ideation, prototyping, and capstone, plus AI for Human-Centered Design, Technology Futures, information visualization, survey methods, web development, data science and analytics, statistics and data analysis, and business intelligence electives.

#### Bentley University — MS Human Factors in Information Design

- **What it adds beyond Evgeniia's background:** Bentley adds a human-factors and experience-design route with strong behavioral science and client-facing applied work, potentially useful for applied AI/UX product research if the AI context is substantive.
- **Best evidence for methods + technical/AI development:** Pilot 014 reports UX, behavioral science, human behavior, human factors, information products, user-interface design, measurable experience design, AI, ethics, emerging technology, and faculty work at the intersection of AI and HCI, including AI-driven interfaces and extended reality.

## 2. New-program impact

The previous five-program shortlist from pilot 011 and the generated application shortlist was UMD, Drexel, UC Berkeley, UT Austin, and Michigan. The expanded landscape adds several serious candidates, but not all solve the central problem of combining advanced UXR, Human-AI/AI-product evaluation, technical/data literacy, and meaningful applied or research structure.

### Strongest newly added candidates compared with the previous shortlist

- **Iowa State** enters the application-readiness shortlist because it adds a dedicated HCI route with usability evaluation, data analysis, programming-related topics, automation/AI-adjacent context, and thesis/creative-component/capstone options. It is not as directly Human-Centered AI-branded as UT Austin or Drexel, but it is structurally stronger than many UX-only programs.
- **Brandeis** enters because it is the strongest online/flexible new option with required research methods, human factors psychology, capstone, and explicit AI for Human-Centered Design plus data/analytics/statistics electives. It is less research-intensive than the top information-school or dedicated HCI options, but it is a practical application-readiness candidate if flexibility matters.
- **Bentley** enters because it combines human factors, behavioral science, measurable experience design, client projects, capstone collaborations, and AI/HCI faculty and emerging-technology context. It is especially relevant as a professional applied UX/product research alternative.
- **UNC Chapel Hill** becomes a strategic alternative rather than a main shortlist entry because the MSIS structure has strong practicum/master’s-paper evidence and HCI/AI/data-science context, but the master’s-level pathway still needs more confirmation than Berkeley, UT Austin, or Michigan.
- **Tufts** becomes a strategic alternative because it is strong for human factors, statistics, analytical methods, and project/thesis work, but less directly aligned with Human-AI or digital AI-product evaluation than the application-readiness group.
- **UC Santa Cruz** becomes a strategic alternative because the capstone and dedicated HCI evidence are strong, but direct AI/HAI evidence is currently limited.
- **Pace, CU Boulder, CCA, SCAD, Kent State, and UMBC** remain outside the main shortlist for different reasons: Pace and SCAD are strong methods/design options but limited in AI evidence; CU Boulder and CCA are stronger in studio/build/emerging-technology than in advanced UXR/HAI evidence; Kent is a useful online UX option but lacks technical/AI and project evidence; UMBC is promising but still needs current course/track confirmation.

### Which new programs enter the application-readiness shortlist

The new programs entering the application-readiness shortlist are **Iowa State**, **Brandeis**, and **Bentley**.

### Which existing programs they displace, if any

They do **not** displace the previous five. Instead, the application-readiness shortlist expands from five to eight because the required section allows a 6–8 program shortlist and because the previous five still have the strongest combined evidence for Evgeniia's target direction.

If the list later must be reduced back to six, the most vulnerable programs would be the ones with the largest unresolved structural gaps: **UT Austin** because applied project/research structure is not yet established in the repository evidence, **Bentley** because technical/AI requirements are still unclear, and **Brandeis** because much of the AI/data depth is elective. That reduction should happen only after the next application-readiness research stage.

## 3. Decision guide

### Dedicated HCI / UX research master's

This path best preserves Evgeniia's UXR identity while adding HCI theory, technical prototyping, usability/evaluation methods, and applied projects. UMD, Drexel, Iowa State, Indiana, UCSC, UCI, DePaul, RIT, Georgia Tech, CMU, and UW are the main examples. The best fit within this path is a dedicated HCI program with non-entry-level methods, programming/data or AI options, and a thesis/capstone structure. UMD, Drexel, and Iowa State currently stand out because they combine methods, technical development, and project or thesis structures with stronger repository evidence than most UX-only options.

### Information-school HCI + AI/data pathway

This path may best support the transition from established UX research into Human-AI, AI-product evaluation, product research, and research-adjacent roles because it can combine HCI, data, AI, information retrieval, social computing, product strategy, and applied project work. Berkeley, UT Austin, Michigan, UNC, and Cornell are the main examples. This path likely fits Evgeniia best overall if she is willing to plan electives carefully and verify access to AI/HCI courses, labs, and capstone/thesis structures.

### Human factors / engineering route

This path is strongest for rigorous human factors, behavioral science, statistics, analytical methods, systems thinking, and product/interface evaluation. Tufts and Bentley are the clearest examples. It can fit Evgeniia if she wants stronger measurement and human-factors credibility, but it may require checking whether the program leans toward digital products and AI systems rather than broader ergonomics, safety, or physical systems.

### Design-school interaction-design route

This path is strongest for portfolio, studio practice, prototyping, concept-to-product execution, visual/interaction design, and creative technology. CCA, SCAD, CU Boulder, and to some extent UCSC are relevant examples. It is less ideal as the primary path if the main goal is Human-AI research and technical/data literacy, unless the program provides substantial research, AI, analytics, or lab access.

### Online UX / user-centered-design route

This path is strongest for flexibility and professional upskilling. Brandeis, Kent State, ASU, DePaul, Drexel, and possibly Bentley are relevant comparisons. It can fit if logistical constraints matter, but it should not be chosen merely to repeat UX fundamentals. Among online/flexible options, Brandeis is currently the most compelling because it combines required research methods and capstone evidence with named AI-for-HCD and analytics/statistics electives.

### Best-fitting path

The best-fitting path is an **information-school HCI + AI/data pathway or a technically substantive dedicated HCI master's**. That combination best matches Evgeniia's need to keep advanced qualitative/mixed-methods UXR strength while adding AI-product evaluation, Human-Centered AI, data/technical fluency, and applied or research outputs. Pure design-school routes and generic online UX routes are less aligned unless they can show non-entry-level methods, AI/data coursework, and a credible capstone/thesis/lab path.
