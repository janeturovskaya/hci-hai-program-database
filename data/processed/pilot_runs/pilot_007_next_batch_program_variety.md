## Batch diversity

The batch includes dedicated HCI/HCI-design programs, information-school UX/HCI pathways, online/flexible UX programs, a Human-AI-adjacent AI program, and a computational social science adjacent program. The strongest immediate HCI candidates appear to be Indiana HCI/d, UMD HCIM, Drexel HCI/UX, UCI MHCID, and RIT HCI MS. The strongest adjacent pathway candidates appear to be Michigan MSI UXRD and UT Austin MSIS.
