# Pilot 013: New candidate evidence batch 1

Scope: official-source evidence collection for six candidate programs added in PR #28. This report does not add programs, rank programs numerically, or assess admissions, deadlines, or tuition.

## University of Maryland, Baltimore County — MS Human-Centered Computing

- **Methods / UXR development:** Strong official support. The HCC MS is framed for UX designers, user researchers, information architects, usability analysts, practitioners, and researchers. Department context names quantitative and qualitative approaches, lab and field data collection, user-centered design, participatory design, design, implementation, and evaluation of usable interactive systems.
- **HAI or AI-product relevance:** Moderate contextual support. An official department story describes HCC master’s students in an AI chatbot project and mentions AI-focused application development; this is project evidence rather than a required curricular pathway.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Moderate support through the department HCC research framing around implementation and quantitative methods; course-level technical depth still needs a catalog check specific to HCC MS requirements.
- **Applied project / research access:** Good support. ISRC describes the HCC master’s as research-based but application-oriented, with ten courses and an MS thesis option.
- **Career relevance:** Strong UX relevance from direct program language naming UX, user research, information architecture, and usability-analysis roles.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Confirm current HCC MS required courses and whether the practitioner track includes a required applied project distinct from the thesis option.

## Iowa State University — MS Human Computer Interaction

- **Methods / UXR development:** Strong support. Official catalog/course evidence includes usability evaluation, evaluation instruments, usability sessions, data analysis, reporting, and HCI research or industry-practice topics.
- **HAI or AI-product relevance:** Moderate support. Catalog context includes computational perception, language parsing, automated systems, and emerging HCI research; this suggests automation/AI relevance but not a dedicated HAI pathway.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Strong support. The interdepartmental HCI catalog references computational-device design and use, programming-related topics, and data analysis in evaluation coursework.
- **Applied project / research access:** Strong support. The MS offers thesis and creative-component options; the capstone route is 27 coursework credits plus a 3-credit capstone project.
- **Career relevance:** Strong direct support. The official MS page says the program prepares students for HCI careers and doctoral study.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Determine which HCI methods and technical courses are required for the MS versus available as electives.

## Tufts University — MS Human Factors Engineering

- **Methods / UXR development:** Strong human-factors methods support. The curriculum includes human factors core, design core, analytical methods, and optional/required research methods areas; program context says students gain experience in the user-centered design process.
- **HAI or AI-product relevance:** Limited direct support. Official pages emphasize human interaction with physical and virtual systems, computer-interface design, product design, assistive technology, and medical devices, but do not foreground AI or HAI.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Strong support. The curriculum includes Applied Behavioral Statistics for Engineering plus analytical/research methods options.
- **Applied project / research access:** Strong support. The MS curriculum includes a project or thesis requirement.
- **Career relevance:** Strong human-factors and interface relevance. Official pages name product design, computer-interface design, ergonomics, workplace safety, and medical devices/systems design.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Clarify the balance of software/HCI coursework versus broader ergonomics, safety, and physical-systems human factors for a student targeting digital UX/HAI roles.

## University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill — MS Information Science

- **Methods / UXR development:** Moderate support. The MSIS directly covers design, implementation, evaluation, and administration of information systems; faculty context includes HCI, design research, participatory design, information seeking, and research methods.
- **HAI or AI-product relevance:** Moderate contextual support. SILS lab/faculty pages include visualization, HCI, machine learning, artificial intelligence, data science, information visualization, and augmented/virtual reality; this is school research context rather than required MSIS evidence.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Moderate-to-strong information-systems support. The MSIS emphasizes analyzing, organizing, representing, and retrieving information and is STEM-designated; faculty/lab context adds data science, machine learning, databases, and visualization.
- **Applied project / research access:** Strong structural support. MSIS students complete a master’s paper or practicum project plus a comprehensive examination.
- **Career relevance:** Strong information-science relevance, but less dedicated UX/HCI role framing than the HCI/HFE/UX degrees.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Verify current MSIS course pathways that are realistically available to master’s students for HCI/UX, AI, visualization, and research-methods depth.

## University of California, Santa Cruz — Human Computer Interaction MS

- **Methods / UXR development:** Strong direct support. The program says students learn and apply user research, HCI methodologies, inclusive design, teamwork, and stakeholder communication.
- **HAI or AI-product relevance:** Limited direct AI support. The program has product/service/experience design and research challenges, but official evidence collected here does not yet show a dedicated HAI or AI-product requirement.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Good direct support for technical prototyping/build skills; quantitative or programming depth needs course-level confirmation.
- **Applied project / research access:** Very strong support. The annual industry-sponsored capstone uses teams of three or four working with corporate or community partners on partner-defined UX product, service, experience-design, or research challenges.
- **Career relevance:** Strong direct UX career framing for product design and UX research.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Check detailed course requirements for programming, analytics, experimentation, and any AI/HCI electives.

## Kent State University — MS User Experience

- **Methods / UXR development:** Strong direct support. Official pages emphasize discovery research, user research, usability testing, prototyping, UX strategy/assessment, information architecture, interaction design, content strategy, and organization of information.
- **HAI or AI-product relevance:** Limited direct support in sources collected. The program is clearly UX-centered but does not currently show AI/HAI content in the official evidence gathered.
- **Technical or quantitative development:** Moderate practical support through prototyping, assessment, IA, and usability testing; programming, analytics, and experimentation depth remain unclear.
- **Applied project / research access:** Unclear. The program is practice-oriented and multidisciplinary, but the sources collected do not establish a required capstone, practicum, thesis, internship, or client project.
- **Career relevance:** Strong direct UX role support, including official career language and an alumni profile of a UX researcher conducting user research studies at Sony PlayStation.
- **Most important remaining uncertainty:** Confirm whether the MS has a required applied project/capstone/internship and identify any AI, analytics, or experimentation coursework.

## Compact comparison

- **Strongest dedicated HCI / human factors option:** Iowa State and UCSC have the strongest direct dedicated-HCI evidence so far; Tufts has the strongest engineering human-factors structure with statistics and project/thesis evidence.
- **Strongest information-science option:** UNC Chapel Hill is the clearest information-science pathway because MSIS directly covers information-system design, implementation, evaluation, information organization/retrieval, and a master’s paper or practicum.
- **Strongest online/flexible option:** Kent State is the clearest online/flexible option because the official iSchool page says all UXD courses are offered online and the MS is 36 credits.
- **Programs that need another targeted check before they can enter the main shortlist:** UMBC needs a current HCC MS course/track requirement check; UNC needs master’s-level HCI/AI course pathway confirmation; UCSC needs detailed course-level technical/AI verification; Kent State needs capstone/applied-project and AI/analytics verification; Tufts needs a software/digital-HCI depth check; Iowa State needs required-versus-elective methods/technical course confirmation.
